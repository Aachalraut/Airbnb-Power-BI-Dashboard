# 📊 Airbnb Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This Power BI project delivers an interactive analysis of Airbnb listings, uncovering patterns in market presence, property types, pricing, and host behavior. Users can dynamically explore the data across dimensions like location, room type, and host profiles.

The report is organized into four analytical themes: **Overview, Property Analysis, Pricing Analysis,** and **Host Analysis** — giving a holistic view of Airbnb's operational landscape.

## 📁 Dashboard Structure
1. Overview
2. Property Analysis
3. Pricing Analysis
4. Host Analysis

---

## 1️⃣ Overview Dashboard
A high-level snapshot of market size and activity.

**Visuals:**
- **KPIs:** Total Listings, Total Hosts, Average Price, Total Reviews
- **Popular Neighbourhoods** (Bar Chart) — demand concentration by area
- **Listings by Neighbourhood Group/Neighbourhood** (Map) — geographic spread of listings

**Insights:**
- Williamsburg (3,910 listings) and Bedford-Stuyvesant (3,700 listings) are the top neighbourhoods by volume, followed by Harlem (2,642 listings)
- Manhattan and Brooklyn hold the highest concentration of listings overall

## 2️⃣ Property Analysis Dashboard
Explores the structure of Airbnb's property and room offerings.

**Visuals:**
- **Room Type Share** (Pie Chart)
- **Room Type by Neighbourhood** (Column Chart)

**Insights:**
- ~25% of listings are entire homes/apartments, ~22% are private rooms, and shared rooms make up only ~1%
- Manhattan and Brooklyn lead in room type diversity and volume — more than double other boroughs

## 3️⃣ Pricing Analysis Dashboard
Examines how pricing varies across location, room type, and popularity.

**Visuals:**
- **Average Price by Neighbourhood Group & Room Type** (Column Chart)
- **Average Price by Room Type** (Donut Chart)
- **Price vs. Number of Reviews** (Scatter Plot)
- **Geographical Price Distribution** (Map)

**Insights:**
- Entire homes/apartments account for ~57% of average pricing share — the highest-value room type
- Most reviewed listings fall under 2,000 reviews, with a concentration between 100–200 reviews
- Manhattan and Brooklyn have the highest average listing prices

## 4️⃣ Host Analysis Dashboard
Analyzes host behavior and its relationship to pricing and popularity.

**Visuals:**
- **Top 15 Hosts by Number of Reviews** (Column Chart)
- **Average Price by Top 30 Hosts & Reviews** (Heatmap)
- **Hosts by Room Type** (Donut Chart)

**Insights:**
- Hosts named Michael and David have the highest review counts, indicating strong guest engagement and trust

---

## ✅ Conclusion
Manhattan and Brooklyn are the most active Airbnb markets, led by neighbourhoods like Williamsburg, Bedford-Stuyvesant, and Harlem. Entire homes and private rooms dominate the market, while shared rooms remain rare. Entire homes command the highest average price, and listings with 100–200 reviews are most common — reflecting guest preference for moderately priced, well-reviewed stays. Top hosts like Michael and David stand out for strong engagement, rounding out a clear picture of Airbnb's pricing, property, and host trends.
